WHAT'S INSIDE

  • REAL PRICES, LIVEEvery quote is the real market price, refreshed every 10 seconds — premarket, regular hours, after hours and overnight. When a stock rips or tanks out there, it rips or tanks in here.
  • TRADE IT LIKE IT'S REALBuy and sell fractional or full shares at the last live price, any session, whenever you want. Line and candle charts from 1 day to 5 years, one candle per bar.
  • VERITY AI PREDICTORAsk the Predictor about any ticker and get a call — Bullish, Bearish, Neutral or Mixed, with a confidence score, the key points and the risks — built from the live quote and fresh news.
  • BUY / SELL PINGSThe engine watches your whole board for you — the core 12 plus everything you add: dips and momentum worth buying, and your positions hitting the −5% stop or +10% target lines. Pings land in the app and, if you set it up, in your email.
