THE GAME

HeyGains starts you with $100,000 of fake cash (or pick your own starting amount) and real live quotes for 12 tickers plus your own stocks and crypto, refreshing every 10 seconds. Trading never closes — you can buy and sell in any session, at the live extended price, never the close. The guardrails are −5% stop / +10% target, the Predictor answers your questions, buy/sell pings watch your lines, and the Auto Trader can run the whole thing 24/7. Paper trading only — no real money, no real orders.

RESET EVERYTHING?

This wipes: your cash back to your starting amount, all positions, buy/sell history, Predictor history, pings and your best run. Other players keep their own games, and your ping settings stay.

START OVER AT $100,000?

This wipes: your cash, all positions, buy/sell history, Predictor history, pings and your best run — and restarts your game at the new amount. Your board, settings, email and theme stay.

DELETE YOUR ACCOUNT?

This permanently deletes your account, positions, trades, settings, pings and Predictor history. You will disappear from the sign-in page. This cannot be undone.
